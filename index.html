Short Biography

Nusaiba Nueri Nasir is a Lecturer at the Institute of Water and Flood Management (IWFM), Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). Prior to joining IWFM as a Lecturer in January 2024, she worked as a Research Assistant at the same institute. She completed her B.Sc. in Water Resources Engineering from the Department of Water Resources Engineering, BUET, in 2023 and is currently pursuing her M.Sc. in Climate Modeling and Risk Management at IWFM. Her academic and research interests include remote sensing and GIS, climate change, hydrology, and risk management.
